# Inspection specification: tea-light candles, tray of four

Part code: `candle`. Final visual inspection from above of four tea lights in their aluminium
cups. Written for this repository as an example specification; the defect classes follow the
VisA annotations for this part.

Verdicts: **REJECT** (scrap or rework), **REVIEW** (hold for human re-inspection), **ACCEPT**.

## CND-GEN-01 Scope and verdict rules
Applies to the wax surface, the wick and the aluminium cup (packaging) of every candle in the tray.
If several findings apply, the most severe verdict wins. Findings that cannot be assigned to any
clause are REVIEW.

## CND-PKG-01 Damaged packaging cup
The aluminium cup must be round with an intact rim. A dented, bent, torn or crushed cup, including
a damaged corner or edge of the packaging, is a major defect. Verdict: REJECT.

## CND-WAX-01 Missing wax
The wax surface must be complete and level. A chunk of wax broken off, a crater, a chip at the edge
or any missing piece of wax is a major defect. Verdict: REJECT.

## CND-WAX-02 Excess wax
Extra wax on the surface, a raised lump or wax built up above the rim is a minor defect.
Verdict: REVIEW.

## CND-WAX-03 Wax spilled out of the cup
Wax that has melted or run out of the cup onto the rim or the outside is a major defect.
Verdict: REJECT.

## CND-COL-01 Colour spot
The wax must be uniformly white. A different-coloured spot, stain or discoloured area on the wax
is a minor defect. Verdict: REVIEW.

## CND-FOR-01 Foreign particle
Any foreign particle, hair, dirt or debris on or embedded in the wax is a major defect.
Verdict: REJECT.

## CND-WCK-01 Abnormal wick
The wick must be centred, upright and of normal length. A missing, bent, off-centre, too long or
otherwise weird wick is a major defect. Verdict: REJECT.
